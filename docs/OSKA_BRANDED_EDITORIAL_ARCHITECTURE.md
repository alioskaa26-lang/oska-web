# OSKA Branded Editorial Architecture — Safe Search Capture

OSKA remains an independent manufacturer and design partner. Third-party brand names may appear only when an article genuinely analyzes a design, material, market or buying topic. Never use competitor names as OSKA product names, collection names, hidden text, doorway pages, fake comparisons or affiliation claims.

## Editorial clusters
- Masculine silver design language: broad market context while centering OSKA manufacturing capability.
- Stone-set luxury techniques: materials and setting concepts only where factually relevant.
- Istanbul jewelry production: CAD, sampling, 925 silver, brass/bronze, finishing, QC and private label.
- Buyer guides: evaluating manufacturers, samples, finishes, reorder readiness and technical briefs.
- Category explainers: signet rings, link bracelets, tennis, chain/mesh and sculptural motifs.

## Third-party brand guardrails
Names such as David Yurman, Cartier, Bulgari/Bvlgari, Messika, Tiffany, John Hardy, Tom Wood, Zen Pırlanta or Atasay may be used only inside independent editorial/comparative content with a real informational purpose. No logos, copied imagery, protected collection names as OSKA products, affiliation language, doorway pages, keyword stuffing or scaled brand-query pages.

## SEO structure
One real user intent per article. Use original OSKA workshop/product media where possible, clear author/date/update fields, internal links to relevant OSKA category/Materials/Manufacturing/RFQ pages, and a project-led CTA rather than a competitor-lookalike sales claim.
