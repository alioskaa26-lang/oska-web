# OSKA Lead Engine Rules

- Türkiye first.
- Brass/Bronze is the current commercial priority; maintain required Silver/other baselines without starving them.
- Contactability is a hard gate: a usable verified communication path is required before CONTACT_READY.
- No contact path means HOLD_CONTACT_GAP, not outreach-ready.
- Deduplicate by normalized company identity and apex domain.
- Keep evidence for fit and contactability; do not inflate counts with weak or unverifiable prospects.
- Do not treat “in Master” as “ready to contact.”
- Outbound email, WhatsApp, DM, or other customer sends remain Human Approval gated.
- Completed verified discovery/enrichment must not be replayed after provider failover.
