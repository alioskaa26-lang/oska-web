# OSKA Creative and CAD Rules

- Preserve the approved product identity first: geometry, material, stone/color logic, proportions, and manufacturability.
- Do not silently swap model, metal, color, stone treatment, gender positioning, or scale.
- Product renders should respect the requested angle/distance; do not make every shot macro.
- When producing a standard product image set, keep outputs as separate usable images unless the task explicitly requests a collage.
- Hero/video work must use approved product assets and preserve the OSKA premium manufacturing story.
- A generation is not accepted from visual plausibility alone when identity/fidelity can be checked against source assets.
- Use canary generation before bulk generation when a new model/provider or workflow is introduced.
- Keep production publish/use behind the applicable Human Approval gate.
